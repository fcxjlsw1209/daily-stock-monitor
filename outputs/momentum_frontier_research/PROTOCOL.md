# Momentum frontier batch 1 — preregistered 2026-10-04
Research only; live ledgers, weights and frozen code unchanged. Goal: explore whether near-50% net CAGR is supported, not optimize indefinitely until a historical number appears.
Default pending risk preference: long-only unlevered ordinary stocks. Existing research engine: max five positions, 20% each, sector max two, next open execution, fixed five-session exit. Not the live 10%-per-position guarded10 strategy.
Momentum family capped at 50% total: R12–1 alone 50%, or R12–1 30% + market-residual momentum 20%. Other factors: return-path continuity, low downside volatility, low jump concentration, CMF. Continuity is used as trend quality rather than additional return lookback; disclose economic overlap with momentum.
Two fixed entry gates: original broad pullback, or positive R12–1 plus above SMA100 in SPY>SMA200 (no pullback requirement). Four fixed rank recipes each, costs 10 and 20 bps roundtrip: 16 runs. No search over arbitrary weights after seeing results.
Sample 2019–2026-09-18; report 2019–21, 2022–23 and 2024–26 separately. All these dates have been explored before and are NOT untouched out-of-sample. Current top-100 universe has survivorship bias; no claim of investable 50% CAGR from this dataset alone. Final verification requires point-in-time universe and genuinely new forward data. No annualizing the brief live sample.
Save all candidates, not only winners. Benchmark QQQ over identical dates and costs. Evaluate CAGR, drawdown, exposure, turnover/trade count, and cost sensitivity. A 50% headline with large drawdown or concentrated recent-period gains is not automatically accepted.
Sources: https://repub.eur.nl/pub/22252/ResidualMomentum-2011.pdf (our lagged market-beta residual is a simplified proxy, not original three-factor implementation); https://academicweb.nd.edu/~zda/Frog.pdf (continuous return paths); https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439.pdf (momentum crash risk, primarily long-short evidence); https://www.nber.org/papers/w5375 (earnings momentum, requires announcement-time data not currently available).
